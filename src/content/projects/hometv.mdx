## Overview

HomeTV is a tvOS app I built for my own living room. It browses Stremio addon catalogs, searches
across them, and plays streams in its own player or hands them to Infuse or VLC.

## The problem

Stremio has no Apple TV app, so on the TV I had to choose between my addons and a native experience.
I wanted something that feels like the Apple TV app but reads from the addons I already use.

## What I did

- Built a Watch Now screen with a hero shelf and one row per addon catalog.
- Wrote a Stremio client that queries every installed addon for catalogs, search and streams.
- Built detail pages with backdrops, synopses and episode lists for series.
- Integrated KSPlayer so MKV and other formats play in-app, falling back from AVPlayer to FFmpeg.
- Added Infuse and VLC hand-off for streams the built-in player can't handle.

## Outcome

HomeTV runs on my Apple TV, sideloaded from Xcode, and isn't on the App Store. Building it taught
me SwiftUI on tvOS, and that designing for a remote and the focus engine is a different job from
designing for touch.
