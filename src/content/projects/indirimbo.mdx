## Overview

Indirimbo puts the Agakiza and Gushimisha Imana hymnbooks on your phone. You can browse by book or
topic, search by title, number or a line you remember, and read every song offline.

## The problem

Churchgoers carry heavy printed hymnals or search for lyrics in WhatsApp groups. Finding a hymn when
you only remember one line is slow, and many churches have weak or no mobile data.

## What I did

- Built one Expo codebase that ships to iOS, Android and the web.
- Added fuzzy search with Fuse.js over titles, numbers and full lyrics.
- Bundled all lyrics in the app so it works with no connection, and shipped content fixes over the air.
- Designed the reading view: adjustable text size, light and dark themes, and swipe between songs.
- Added favourites, recent songs and share links that open a song directly.

## Outcome

Indirimbo is live on the App Store, Google Play and the web, from a single codebase. It taught me
how to ship one React Native app to three platforms, and how far over-the-air updates go in keeping
content fresh between store releases.
