## Overview

Umuhanda helps people in Rwanda prepare for the provisional driving licence exam. It has the full
question bank in Kinyarwanda, English and French, a practice mode with explanations, and a timed
mock exam.

## The problem

Most learners study from printed booklets or scattered PDFs, often in a language they're less
comfortable reading. The official exam is timed, so practising without a clock leaves people
unprepared. Many learners also study on cheap phones with patchy data.

## What I did

- Built a trilingual question bank of road rules and road signs, with Kinyarwanda as the default.
- Built an untimed practice mode with instant feedback, and a 20-minute exam that mirrors the real one.
- Wrote client-side search with stemming and question-number lookup, with no server round trips.
- Made it an installable PWA that works offline, with an optional road-sign image pack.
- Pre-rendered every page with structured data and `hreflang` so each language ranks on its own.

## Outcome

Umuhanda is live with 198 questions in three languages, all usable offline. It taught me how
much of a site's quality lives in the unglamorous parts: redirects, caching headers, and keeping
three translations of every question in sync.
