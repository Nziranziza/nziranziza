## Overview

1000hills is a mobile app for exploring Rwanda's museums and heritage sites from anywhere. Guides
and curators post artefacts with photos and video, and visitors browse them in a feed. Virtual
tours and an AR viewer are next.

## The problem

Rwanda's heritage sites hold a lot of history, but most of it is only accessible if you can travel
there. The people who know the stories best, guides and curators, have no simple way to share them
with a wider audience.

## What I did

- Led mobile development and designed the app in Figma, from sign-in to the post composer.
- Built the React Native app with Expo Router: feed, search, notifications and profiles.
- Built image and video uploads so guides can publish artefacts with photos and clips.
- Prototyped AR scenes with ViroReact for viewing artefacts in your own space.
- Connected the app to a Node.js API with Firebase authentication.

## Status & next steps

Sign-up, the feed, profiles and posting work. Next are the virtual tours themselves and the AR
viewer, then real content from heritage sites.
