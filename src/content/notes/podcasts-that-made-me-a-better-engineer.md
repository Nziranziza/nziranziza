I listen to podcasts on the way to work and while doing the dishes. Most of what I
know outside my day job I picked up this way: one engineer explaining a decision to another. These
are the three I keep coming back to.

## React Native Radio

[React Native Radio](https://open.spotify.com/show/28hPRLml3FbP14FCYtlOvg) is hosted by engineers
who have worked with React Native since its early days. I build most of my apps with it, and this
show helped me understand the platform underneath: native modules, the new architecture, and when a
hybrid or mobile web app is the better choice. It made me a better mobile engineer, not just a
better React Native user.

## Backend Banter

[Backend Banter](https://open.spotify.com/show/35trT95UkRVCkEb6tXndpF) is Lane Wagner's show about
backend development. Lane is a big advocate of Go, and before this podcast I barely knew the
language. Hearing guests compare Go, Rust and other strongly typed languages showed me where
JavaScript fits among them, and where it doesn't.

## PodRocket

[PodRocket](https://open.spotify.com/show/6oFuKu89C9X1wQ7bT0QEM2) is LogRocket's web development
podcast. Each episode is a conversation with someone building a framework, a tool or a browser
feature. It's how I keep up with the web without reading every changelog.
